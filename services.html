Services

Types of data entry work handled on the portal

Student Academic Records

Class 10th and 12th student details from school registers – name, parents, date of birth, roll number, board and percentage.

School Survey Forms

Area-wise survey of schools and enrolled students, entered village by village or ward by ward.

Scholarship Applications

Digitising scholarship application forms received in batches so they can be processed faster.

Workflow

What happens to every entry

StageWhoWhat happensStatus shown
1. AssignmentSuper AdminAssigns an area, task type, target and rate to a DEONew
2. EntryDEOFills the entry form for each record in the assigned areaPending
3. VerificationVerifierChecks the entry against the source and approves or rejects itApproved / Rejected
4. CorrectionDEOReads the rejection reason, corrects and resubmits the entryPending
5. PayoutAdminApproved entries × rate are paid between 15th – 25th of the monthPaid
