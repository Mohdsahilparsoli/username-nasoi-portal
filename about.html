About NASOI

Who we are and what this portal does

Our mission

National Academic Services of India (NASOI) is a demo project that models how academic records – student results, school surveys and scholarship applications – can be digitised by a distributed team of Data Entry Operators working from home.

Every record entered by an operator is checked by a Verifier before it is accepted. Operators are paid only for verified, accurate entries, which keeps data quality high and payments fair and transparent.

What makes the process reliable

  • Area-wise work allocation by the Super Admin
  • Two-level process: entry by DEO, check by Verifier
  • Clear rejection reasons so operators can correct mistakes
  • Live earnings and month-wise payout history

Eligibility

  • Class 10th (Matriculation) passed from a recognised board
  • Own Android mobile / tablet / laptop / desktop
  • Reliable internet connection
  • A bank account in your own name for payouts

Work terms

  • Part-time, contractual, work from home
  • Entries only through this portal
  • Payout between 15th – 25th of each month
