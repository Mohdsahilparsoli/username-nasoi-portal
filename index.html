How it works

Four simple steps from registration to payout.

1

Register

Fill the registration form with personal, address and bank details. You instantly get an Employee ID and password.

2

Get assigned

The Super Admin assigns you an area and task type with a target number of entries.

3

Enter data

Add entries online from your dashboard. Each entry goes to a Verifier for checking.

4

Earn

Every approved entry adds to your earnings. See totals and month-wise history anytime.

Three roles, one portal

Data Entry Operator (DEO)

  • Profile & banking details
  • Assigned area & targets
  • Pending / approved / rejected entries
  • Rejection reason view
  • Total earnings & monthly history

Verifier (VR)

  • Queue of entries to verify
  • Approve with one click
  • Reject with a reason
  • Approved / rejected history

Super Admin

  • View all operators
  • Assign area & task to a DEO
  • Set rate per entry
  • Monitor all entries & payouts
